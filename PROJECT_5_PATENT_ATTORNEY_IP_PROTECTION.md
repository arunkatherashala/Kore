## 1. Patent Filing Strategy

### Patents to File

#### Patent 1: Hybrid Compression Algorithm
**Title**: "Adaptive Compression Method for Structured Data with Context-Aware Selection"

**Invention Claims**:
- Dynamic selection between RLE, LZ77 (LZSS), dictionary, FOR compression
- Context-aware compression based on data characteristics
- Hybrid model achieving 40-70% compression ratios
- Real-time performance optimization

**Technical Highlights**:
```
- Novel scoring function for algorithm selection
- Parallel compression evaluation
- Metadata preservation
- Streaming decompression support
```

**Prior Art Analysis**:
- ✅ Different from: gzip, brotli, zstd
- ✅ Novel aspects: Context-aware hybrid approach
- ✅ Potential claims: 15-20 claims of varying breadth

**Estimated Timeline**: 3-5 years prosecution
**Estimated Cost**: $5,000 - $8,000 (US only)

- ❌ Limited prosecution experience
- ❌ May lack international expertise

**Cost**: $5,000 - $12,000 per patent
