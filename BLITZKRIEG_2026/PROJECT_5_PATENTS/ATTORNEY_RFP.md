## 🗣️ INTERVIEW QUESTIONS FOR PATENT ATTORNEYS

**During initial call, ask:**

1. **Technical Expertise**
   - Have you worked with compression algorithm patents?
   - Experience with data format patents (like Parquet)?
   - Familiar with Rust, C++, or systems programming?

2. **Timeline & Capacity**
   - Can you commit 40-50 hours in 10 days?
   - Will you be the primary attorney on this?
   - What's your typical turnaround for provisional patents?

3. **Process & Quality**
   - Will you interview our technical team?
   - How many back-and-forth iterations?
   - What's included in your quote ($25K)?

4. **References**
   - Can you provide 2-3 reference clients?
   - Have you worked with other compression tech companies?
   - Any data format patent experience?

5. **Cost Breakdown**
   - Is $25K fixed or estimate?
   - What if we want to convert to utility patents later?
   - International filing (PCT) costs?
